# Mascot — Work in Progress (paused 2026-10-11)

Exploration of a Duolingo-style mascot for findraco: a baby dragon guarding his gold hoard,
with a future role as guide for financial-literacy lessons (see `IDEAS.md` §10). Paused by the
user — none of the generated options was approved. Reference images live in `mascot/`.

## Decisions so far

- **The app palette stays amber/charcoal.** The mascot's teal is NOT adopted as a UI brand color.
  If the mascot returns, its colors must fit the existing palette, not the other way round.
- The current coin logo is kept (refined) until the mascot is settled.

## Direction learned

- Personality: D&D-inspired — intelligent, cunning, greedy about gold; "greedy on your behalf"
  (hates seeing you waste money), mischievous, never scary. Plain "cute" was rejected as bland.
- Flat geometric, outline-free style (`04`–`06`) read far more distinctive than the outlined
  sticker style (`02`, `03`).
- Face carries the character: golden half-lidded eyes, raised brow, side smirk, single fang.
- Best moments: scheming chin-tap with coin, angry reading a receipt, sleeping on the hoard.

## Open problems

- Round head + upward crescent horns reads as a devil/😈, especially at icon size. Needs dragon
  cues: short snout with nostrils, back-swept horns, rounded spine ridge.
- Proportions drift between images; needs a locked model sheet before any icon derivation.
- Coin-shaped chest scale (signature detail) got lost in later rounds.
- Not yet tested against the amber/charcoal palette.
